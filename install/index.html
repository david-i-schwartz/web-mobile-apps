Put DIS on Your Home Screen

DIS is Dave’s Interesting Stuff: every app and game behind one icon. Add it once, and it opens like any other app on your iPhone or iPad.

Which Screen Looks Like Yours?

Open this page in Safari. Look at Safari’s bar of buttons: it is at the bottom on an iPhone and at the top on an iPad. Then tap the picture that matches.

Screen With a Share Button

Watch it through once first: the list of options covers this page while you work.

  1. Tap Share, the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bar at the bottom.
  2. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, make sure it is switched on. Leave the name as DIS.
  4. Tap Add.

DIS is now on your Home Screen. Open it from there from now on.

No Add to Home Screen in the list? Scroll to the very bottom, tap Edit Actions, then tap the plus beside Add to Home Screen.

Screen With a Tabs Button

Watch it through once first: the list of options covers this page while you work.

  1. Tap the small button at the left end of the address bar. Apple calls it the Page Menu.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, make sure it is switched on. Leave the name as DIS.
  5. Tap Add.

DIS is now on your Home Screen. Open it from there from now on.

Another way to reach Share: press and hold the address bar, then tap Share.

Steps for an iPad

Watch it through once first: the list of options covers part of this page while you work.

  1. Tap Share, the square with an arrow pointing up, at the top right beside the address bar.
  2. Tap View More. It may say More. If the list is already long, skip this step.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, make sure it is switched on. Leave the name as DIS.
  5. Tap Add.

DIS is now on your Home Screen. Open it from there from now on.

No Add to Home Screen in the list? Scroll to the very bottom, tap Edit Actions, then tap the plus beside Add to Home Screen.

Steps for Chrome

Watch it through once first: the list of options covers this page while you work.

  1. Tap Share, the square with an arrow pointing up, at the right end of Chrome’s address bar.
  2. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, make sure it is switched on. Leave the name as DIS.
  4. Tap Add.

DIS is now on your Home Screen. Open it from there from now on.

Address bar at the bottom? Chrome lets you move it there. Share is still at its right end. On an iPad, it is the same: Share is to the right of the address bar.

No Add to Home Screen in the list? Scroll to the very bottom, tap Edit Actions, then tap the plus beside Add to Home Screen.

Open This Page in Safari First

Gmail, Facebook, Instagram, the Google app and some others open links inside themselves. Their list of options has no Add to Home Screen, so the page has to move to Safari.

  1. Find that app’s menu button: three dots or a compass, in a corner of the screen.
  2. Tap it, then tap Open in Safari. It may say Open in Browser or Open in External Browser.
  3. No menu? Copy this page’s link, open Safari, and paste the link into the address bar.
  4. When this page opens in Safari, pick the picture that matches your screen.

Using Chrome? It has its own steps

Using Edge or Firefox? Look in that browser’s menu for Share or Add to Home Screen. Or open this page in Safari and follow the pictures.

Good to Know
  • Saves stay where they were made. A game you played in Safari or Chrome keeps its save in that browser, and the DIS icon starts fresh. It is best to add DIS before you start playing. To bring saves across: open DIS in the browser you played in and tap Back Up My Saves, then open DIS from the Home Screen and tap Restore.
  • Just want one app? Open that app’s own page and do the same taps there. It gets its own icon, with its own saves, separate from DIS.
  • Got the link in an email or a message? Mail and Messages open links in your usual browser, Safari or Chrome, so you can go straight to the steps. Gmail, Facebook and Instagram keep links inside themselves: use Open This Page in Safari First.
  • Everything stays on your device. Nothing you make or play is uploaded.
