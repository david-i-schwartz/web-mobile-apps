Hex bead cubes

Tumbling-block cubes for a hexagonal pegboard. Isolate a colour, tap beads off as you place them, and build one cube at a time. Progress is saved in this browser.

This browser can’t save right now (its storage is full or switched off). You can keep working, but changes made from now on won’t be remembered after you close the page.

Inspect is for looking. Tap a bead to see its colour, coordinate and cube under the board. Nothing gets ticked off, and you can mark, clear or focus on that bead’s whole cube from there.

Mark placed is for building. Tap a bead to tick it off as placed, and tap it again to undo.

With a keyboard, the arrow keys move the selection and Space ticks the selected bead in either mode.
