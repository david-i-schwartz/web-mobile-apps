SITE: Site Shortcuts

One tap to the right Pages CMS form for david-i-schwartz.github.io: add a paper, news, a post or photos.

This browser isn’t keeping your work between visits.

How It Works

  1. The first time, Pages CMS asks you to sign in with GitHub. After that, every button opens the form straight away.
  2. Fill in the form and press Save. Your site rebuilds and the change is live in about 2 minutes.
  3. Not showing yet? Tap Build Status: a green tick means it’s live, a red cross means something needs fixing.

This app only opens links. It doesn’t sign in, store passwords or send anything anywhere.
