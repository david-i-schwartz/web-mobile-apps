שבת

Seventh

A platformer about rest

Six days you gather manna on the way home. Every seventh stone, the world slows. On the seventh day, you stop.

Move with ← → or A D. Jump with ↑ or Space. Sit with ↓. Esc pauses.
On a phone, turn sideways for the widest view.

How the mechanic teaches
  • Every seventh platform is a resting stone. Landing on it slows the world for a breath, but the sun keeps moving. Rest costs time.
  • Manna melts by midday, and whatever you carry home measures out to an omer: enough. Extra spoils overnight (Exodus 16).
  • On the sixth day twice as much falls, and sunset comes on time whether or not you are home.
  • On the seventh day the clock, the count and the week strip disappear. Nothing falls. Standing still makes the garden bloom, and seven stones ask a question when you sit.
  • Shabbat ends at three stars, not when you are finished.

Erev Shabbat

“And on the seventh day God finished His work which He had made; and He rested on the seventh day from all His work which He had made.”Genesis 2:2

Set the work down. Two candles, for two words: remember and keep.

Three stars

Shabbat ends with havdalah, “separation”: wine, sweet spices and a braided candle mark the line between the holy day and the six days of work.

Shavua tov. A good week.
