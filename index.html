Web & Mobile Apps

Small apps that run in any browser, on a phone, tablet or computer.

Instructions

Tap an app to open it. To keep one on your phone or tablet, add it to your Home Screen: it then opens like any other app, with its own icon, and works without a connection.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open the app in Safari. If the link opened inside Messages, Mail, Gmail or another app, tap or and choose Open in Safari first.
  2. Tap Share. No Share button? Tap first. On an iPad, it’s at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll down to see it.
  4. Tap Add.

Best done before you start: on an iPhone or iPad, the Home Screen app keeps its own copy of your work, separate from Safari’s.

Android
  1. Open the app in Chrome. If the link opened inside another app, tap and choose Open in Chrome first.
  2. Tap (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install or Add.

If the app shows an Install button at the bottom, tapping it does the same.

Computer
  1. Open the app and bookmark it.
  2. In Chrome or Edge you can also install it as an app, from the browser’s menu.

On a phone, each app also shows the steps for that phone the first time it opens. Everything runs on your device: pictures and work you make are never uploaded. When an app is updated, you get the new version the next time you open it online.

Crafts

Pattern makers for fuse beads, bricks and tiles.

FIG: Fusebead Image GeneratorTurn an image into a bead pattern on square, hex or iso pegboards. Isolate a colour, tap beads off as you place them, and work one chunk at a time. Progress is saved in this browser. You can also edit the pattern by hand, design one on an empty board, and keep several patterns. BIG: Brick Image GeneratorTurn a picture into a brick mosaic on real LEGO® baseplates and plates, flat or raised in relief. Choose the parts, join studs into bigger pieces, and get a parts list. Everything stays in this browser. TIG: Tile Image GeneratorTurn a picture into a tile mosaic on mesh sheets, then follow it tile by tile. Everything stays in this browser. PIL: Pegboard Iso LabDesign 3D-illusion bead patterns: isometric shapes, tessellations, seeded terrain and pipe tiles, on a pegboard. HEX: Hex Bead CubesTumbling-block cube patterns for a hexagonal pegboard, marked off bead by bead as you build. ISO: Iso Bead DesignerStack cubes and read off a bead chart for a 48 × 38 isometric pegboard.
