SHOP: Shopping List

A quick shopping list for one phone: add things fast, check them off in the store, and add the usual things back with one tap. Your list stays on this device.

This browser isn’t keeping your work between visits.

My List

Your shopping list. It stays on this phone: nothing is uploaded, and it works with no signal.

  • Type something and tap Add (or press Enter). Add several at once with commas: milk, eggs, bread.
  • Got a paper list? Point Google Lens at it, tap Text, then Select all and Copy, and paste in the box. Each line becomes an item; bullets, boxes and numbers are left off.
  • As you type, things you’ve added before show up under the box. Tap one to add it.
  • Tap an item to check it off. It moves to In the Cart. Tap it again to put it back.
  • × takes an item off the list. Undo takes back the last change.

    Nothing on the list yet. Type something above, or tap a Buy Again item below.

    In the Cart

      Buy Again

      Things you’ve added before, the ones you add most often first. Tap one to put it back on the list. Anything on the list or in the cart isn’t shown here.

      Typo or something you’ll never buy again? Tap Remove from Buy Again, then tap it.

      Everything you add shows up here afterwards, so next time it’s one tap.
