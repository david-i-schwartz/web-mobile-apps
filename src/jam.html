JAM: Jam Cutter

Finds where the band is playing in a practice recording, cuts each take into MP3s, and lets you name, keep or toss them. Everything stays on this device.

This browser isn’t keeping your work between visits.

Cut a Practice

What This Does

JAM listens to a practice recording, finds where the band is playing (not talking or tuning), and cuts each take into its own MP3. Long takes are also cut into parts where the sound changes, such as a new riff or a break.

Getting the Recording Here

  • After practice, in Google Recorder, tap Share, then Drive, and save it to S&W / Jams / Inbox.
  • Or download it from recorder.google.com into that folder.

Two Ways to Work

  • Folders (a computer with Chrome or Edge, and Google Drive for desktop): JAM finds new recordings in the Inbox, cuts them, and saves the cuts in the Jams folder. Drive for desktop uploads them.
  • One recording (any phone or computer): choose a recording, review the cuts, then share them to Drive or save a zip.

Nothing is uploaded by JAM itself, and your original recordings are never deleted.

    What This Is

    Listen to each cut, name the keepers, and toss the rest. Nothing changes in your folders until you press Apply.

    The Strip

    • The grey shape is how loud the recording is. Shaded areas are takes, thin lines are where parts start, and the dashed line is the level that counts as playing.
    • Tap or click the strip to play from that moment.

    Clips

    • A long take comes as the full take plus its parts, so nothing is only available chopped up.
    • A name turns the file into recording - name.mp3. Blank keeps the file name.
    • Song files a copy into that song's folder in New Songs.

    Keys

    • J next clip, K previous, Space play or pause, X toss or keep.

    Advanced

    These are used for the next cut. The defaults are the ones Jam Cutter 1.1 uses.

    Lower catches quieter playing; higher ignores more talking and noodling.

    Below 1 finds more parts, above 1 fewer.
